How the ratings work
  • Pressure: a win-probability model scores every ball; leverage is how much the outcome of that ball can swing the win chance. The top 15% by leverage in each phase and innings are pressure balls (knockouts ×1.5, super overs automatic).
  • Runs above average (RAA): on each pressure ball, runs minus what an average player would score in the same situation, minus the run value of a dismissal. Bowlers are the mirror image.
  • Pressure rating: RAA per 100 pressure balls, weighted by phase, opposition and season, then shrunk toward the player's own long-run record so small samples cannot top the list by luck.
  • Final score: 0.8 × pressure rating + 0.2 × recent form (2025–26), both in runs above average per 100 balls.
  • Uncertainty: ranks 1–3 are well supported; ranks 4–10 are close calls. The resample column shows how often a player stays in the top 10 when matches are resampled 200 times.
Data: IPL and T20I ball-by-ball records, 2022–2026, supplied for SupeRR Selector Season 2. Built with Python (pandas, scikit-learn, LightGBM) and D3.
